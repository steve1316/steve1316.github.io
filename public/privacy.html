Privacy Policy

job-hunter is a personal tool run by Steve Tu for his own job search. It is not offered to anyone else.

It connects only to Steve Tu's own Google account. It reads Gmail messages about job applications, writes to one Google Sheet that tracks applications, and saves resume and cover letter drafts to one Google Drive folder.

The data is kept on Steve Tu's own computer and in his own Google account. To sort job emails and write drafts, the text of those emails and of job postings is sent to Anthropic's Claude. The data is not sold or shared with anyone else.
